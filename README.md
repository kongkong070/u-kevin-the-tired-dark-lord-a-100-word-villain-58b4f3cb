A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.
The Hollow King has drowned a coastal city every dawn for three centuries —
but tonight the tide tables arrived in triplicate, the phylactery hums like a
kettle, and the hero has come to kill him. The tiredness lives in the pauses,
the counting, and the small deflections; it is never announced outright.

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.
